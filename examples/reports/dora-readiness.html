Method, sources and caveats

Traffic lights – register: red with more than 10 validation errors, amber with 1–10; contracts: red with high-priority gaps or fewer than half of the critical arrangements assessed, amber with any gap, exception or assessment outstanding; incidents: red with an overdue report or a recurring series meeting the major criteria, amber with pending reports or undetermined classifications; concentration: red when one group has ≥ 25 % of spend, groups support ≥ 5 critical functions, a critical function depends on a single hard-to-substitute provider or exit plans cover < 90 %; amber for moderate HHI, single providers, incomplete exit plans, ≥ 20 % third-country spend or hidden concentration. The overall status is the worst of these four; FINMA coverage is informational. Sources: the register as reported, the Art. 30 contract assessments and the incident log maintained in the toolkit.

Status per provision: present, partial or missing, recorded by the assessor with the clause reference and evidence. Art. 30(3) applies when the register links the arrangement to a critical or important function (B_02.02 → B_06.01). Call-offs inherit unassessed items from their overarching arrangement (B_02.01 c0030); such gaps are fixed – and listed – once, in the overarching arrangement. Priority: missing clause on an arrangement supporting a critical function = high; partial clause on such an arrangement or missing clause elsewhere = medium; partial clause elsewhere = low. Proposed target dates: high 90 days, medium 180 days, low 365 days from the assessment that found the gap (the plan date if undated) – align them with renewal dates where earlier; a target date agreed for a gap replaces the proposal.

Classification per DORA Art. 18 and the RTS on classification (Delegated Regulation (EU) 2024/1772): major when critical services are affected and either a successful malicious unauthorised access occurred or two or more of the other six criteria meet their thresholds. Unknown answers are evaluated both ways; the outcome is definitive only when they cannot change it. Reporting deadlines per Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/301 without weekend relief. Thresholds and article numbers are open assumptions (A-INC-*).
